Content block — a flat white surface (hairline, no shadow) or one solid saturated fill. Never gradients.

```jsx
<Card variant="orange" eyebrow="Attraction" title="Immersive phygital"
      action={<Button variant="secondary" chevron>Learn more</Button>}>
  Blend physical play with digital interaction.
</Card>
```

Variants: `surface` (default), `orange`, `dark`, `lilac`, `amber`. Slots: `eyebrow`, `title`, `children`, `action`. Radius is lg (32px).
